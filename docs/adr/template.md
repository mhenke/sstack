## Context

What forced a decision. The constraint, the prior art, the trade-off.
Two or three paragraphs at most; this is not a design doc.

## Decision

The choice, in the active voice: "We will …". One decision per ADR.
If the ADR has an "and" in its title, it is two ADRs.

## Consequences

**Good**: what this buys.

**Bad**: what it costs, honestly. A decision with no recorded cost
was not a real decision.

**Risks**: what could prove it wrong, and what would trigger a
revisit.
